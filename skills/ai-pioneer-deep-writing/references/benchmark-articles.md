# Benchmark Articles

Use these two articles as quality benchmarks for AI Pioneer deep writing:

- 数字生命卡兹克, `聊聊Anthropic这篇最新研究，我觉得可能是AI意识诞生的前夜。`
  https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Qsh68u0yFOTuJtBXNVKH0w
- AGI Hunt, `刚刚，Anthropic 宣布：Claude 自己长出了意识`
  https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/TVMrFGWsPvLOYH8mgZnxeg

Do not copy wording, titles, metaphors, profanity, or personal style. Use them as structural benchmarks.

## What Makes Them Work

1. **They treat the research as a cognitive event**
   - They are not merely explaining `J-space`.
   - They first create a larger question: whether the old “next-token predictor” story still explains what readers are seeing.
   - The research becomes evidence in a bigger shift, not the whole article.

2. **They build historical contrast**
   - Old story: language models as probability machines / stochastic parrots.
   - Middle step: chain-of-thought made reasoning visible, but only as external text.
   - New step: internal representations can be read and sometimes changed before output.
   - This history gives the article depth and makes the reader feel a worldview update.

3. **They use metaphor before terminology**
   - They explain unfamiliar concepts with a memorable image, then introduce the technical term.
   - Use fresh metaphors fit for the topic. Do not reuse their exact “room/ocean” devices unless the user explicitly asks.

4. **They write experiments as a sequence**
   - First show the internal signal can be read.
   - Then show the model can report or control it.
   - Then show intervention changes output.
   - Then show safety implications.
   - This creates a detective-story rhythm.

5. **They delay caveats until after attention is earned**
   - They do not open by neutralizing the story.
   - They let the reader feel why the finding is strange, then add boundaries.
   - For AI Pioneer, keep factual caution, but do not make caution the opening emotion.

6. **They use visual proof**
   - Images correspond to article beats: research cover, concept diagram, experiment output, intervention result, safety case, expert/caveat.
   - A good image answers “where did this claim come from?”

7. **They have shareable sentences**
   - The best lines compress an argument into a sentence readers can screenshot.
   - They are concrete and slightly surprising, not generic industry-speak.

## Minimum Quality Comparison

When finishing a deep article, compare against the two benchmarks on these axes:

| Axis | Benchmark-Level Standard | Fix If Weak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | Opens with a human-readable shock, question, or scene within 5 paragraphs | Replace abstract intro with a concrete situation or contradiction |
| Worldview | Shows which old belief is being updated | Add historical context or prior mainstream explanation |
| Evidence Chain | Evidence escalates step by step | Reorder facts into observation -> intervention -> result |
| Technical Clarity | Terms are accurate but explained through concrete images | Add one precise metaphor and remove jargon stacks |
| Reader Pull | Short paragraphs, turns, questions, and forward motion | Split long paragraphs and add section turns |
| Image Depth | Images prove claims, not decorate | Add official/paper/product/X screenshots for each major beat |
| Caveat | Boundaries are present without killing the story | Move caveats after the reader understands why it matters |
| Shareability | At least 4 sentences can stand alone as screenshots | Rewrite bland claims into concrete, risky-but-accurate lines |
| Reader Value | Ends with a practical lens or observation variables | Add what developer/founder/operator/reader should watch next |
| Human Landing | Ending returns to a person, a use case, or a lived unease | Replace abstract elevation with a scene that echoes the opening |

If an article scores below benchmark on any major axis, revise before upload.

## Quantitative Targets

Use these as defaults for complex research/event pieces:

- 4500-7500 Chinese characters, unless the user explicitly requests short.
- 10+ images for benchmark-level depth; 6 is the floor.
- At least 70% images from real sources.
- Average paragraph length under 45 Chinese characters where possible.
- At least one third of paragraphs should be short pauses or turns.
- 8+ verifiable fact anchors.
- 5+ source links.
- 2+ historical parallels or prior narrative anchors.

These numbers do not guarantee quality, but failing many of them usually indicates the draft is thin.

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Starting with “X 发布了 Y” unless the fact itself is shocking.
- Opening with “真正值得关注的是” or “如果只看成 X 就小了”.
- Treating official caveats as the whole article.
- Using images as visual filler.
- Writing a linear summary of the paper.
- Calling something consciousness/sentience unless the source explicitly supports that wording and the caveat is clear.
- Inventing inner thoughts for founders, researchers, models, users, or investors.
- Showing the writer's drafting process: “this could be written as…”, “but that would be too rough…”, “more accurately…”.
- Ending with empty elevation: “this marks a new stage”, “this will reshape the future”, “the significance is profound”.
